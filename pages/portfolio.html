About

Building Practical Technology Skills

Hopecore is my personal information technology portfolio. It brings together projects I have created while developing skills in networking, cybersecurity, databases, system administration, and technical troubleshooting.

My projects focus on creating tools that are understandable to people who are still learning technology while also demonstrating concepts that appear in real IT environments.

The goal of this portfolio is to demonstrate not only the finished projects, but also the technical concepts, troubleshooting methods, design decisions, and development processes behind them.
